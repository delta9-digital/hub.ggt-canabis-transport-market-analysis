Vehicle Investment: Market Analysis
Seed to sale, we never fail
Which van should GGT buy next: fresh-frozen reefer capacity for cultivators, or ambient capacity for the hemp beverage and manufactured goods market.
October 5, 2026
The question
Cultivation is the only license type that needs fresh-frozen cold chain. Almost everything else moves ambient.
Fresh-frozen cultivation
Needs a true reefer, 0°F to -20°F. Higher upfront cost, ongoing maintenance, and a narrow customer base.
LPHE cans & cannabis manufactured goods
Moves at ambient temperature. Needs weight capacity and pallet room, not refrigeration.
This deck sizes three transport markets against two vehicle types, so we can pick the next van with the numbers in front of us.

16
Recommendation
Buy a high-roof cargo van for LPHE and cannabis dry goods first
Why now
  • 6x the account base of cannabis, uncapped and still growing
  • Year-round demand, no seasonal bottleneck
  • Our transporter license already covers it, no new endorsement
  • $14.5K–$37.5K used (with CannaCage) vs. $27.5K–$80K for a reefer path
  • Only needs a 1.5–2% share of a 2,015-account market
Hold the reefer for now
  • Fresh-frozen is a handful of accounts inside a 24-license tier
  • Cultivator harvest volume bunches seasonally for outdoor growers
  • A reefer sitting idle most of the year is the wrong first bet
  • Revisit once 3–5 fresh-frozen accounts are signed and ready to ship
